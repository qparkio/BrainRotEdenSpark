## Recorded audio sources

The shipped Kenney effect mapping, transformations, levels, and SHA-256 values are recorded in [`recorded-sfx-sources.json`](../assets/sounds/recorded-sfx-sources.json). Original source packs:

- [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)

The Thunder music source, processing, and hash are in [`thunder-music-source.json`](../assets/sounds/thunder-music-source.json), with the [original source page](https://opengameart.org/content/desert-theme) retained in the accompanying notice.

The CC0 notices above apply to those specific files. They do not establish rights for the unrelated legacy MP3 collection. Check or replace unverified audio and the earlier font before relying on a general redistribution or commercial-use license.
